### `workflow-driver-typed-fleet`: build the workflow driver's typed per-unit fleet on the compact Q-58 carrier (`Q-82`, Stage 2)

This step implements the existing Stage-2 architecture; it does not redesign it. The authoritative scope is the Stage 2 paragraph in `docs/plans/agent-scaffold.steps/workflow-driver.md`, with the detailed build path in `docs/plans/mealy-workflow-driver.explorations/r2-synthesis.md` and `r2-architecture-build-path.md`. Q-82 schedules it after the independent visibility repair.

DEPENDENCIES ARE LOAD-BEARING. `structured-risk-class-source`, reached through direct `workflow-loop-visibility` and independently through `review-loop-foreclosure-enforcement`, establishes exact increment ids, Roadmap-increment/task plan declaration variants plus exact-step historical mappings, phase-bearing active selectors, escalation phase, and both bounded historical classifications. `workflow-loop-visibility` completes the typed task/question/step active-work union, phase-correct plural projection, and pending compatibility fallback without forking those selectors. `q58-output-ablation` must select the compact carrier, and `resume-state-currency-signal` must add Q-59's currency value to it. `review-loop-foreclosure-enforcement` must land the reviewed `ReviewProcessHistory` reconstruction shared by W3 and `next`, including phase-preserving round/escalation windows, declaration and snapshot matching, exact selected identity, streak, convergence-first precedence, foreclosure/cap, recheck-capable single passes, and typed historical records. The TOML `blocked_by` edges preserve those prerequisites: visibility and foreclosure are direct, and Q-58 is transitive through `resume-state-currency-signal`. This step consumes those reviewed substrates; it does not assume structured-only before the experiment, restore a prose echo, or extract a second declaration join, selector, process classifier, or loop formula.

THE SCOPE. Extend the shared reconstruction with the Stage-2 `src/driver/` fleet: a disjoint typed `ReviewProcess`, nested `TaskMachine`, `QuestionMachine`, and `StepMachine`, full reconstruction of every typed active unit, and instruction emission from typed state. The fleet may wrap or enrich the earlier result, but W3, `next`, and the fleet call the same implementation for record order, phase-bearing escalation/adoption segments, authoritative declaration lookup, historical classification, snapshot agreement, active identity selection, streak, cap, foreclosure, and convergence/single-pass classification. Judgments enter only through typed inputs; no transition manufactures a triage verdict, risk class, or human decision. Preserve `workflow-loop-visibility`'s complete task/question/step collection, exact phase/identity, pending compatibility fallback, and one selected action until the scheduler deliberately replaces only that fallback.

THE PROCESS TYPES. `ReviewProcess` is an enum with no common bag of optional convergence fields:

- `ReviewProcess::Convergence(ReviewLoop)` accepts only `ConvergencePhase::{PlanReview, WorkReview}` and a resolving authoritative declaration. `ReviewLoop` alone stores `risk_class`, round count, consecutive/required streak, foreclosure, cap segment, and convergence/escalation status. Its total transition function applies the shared arithmetic and high/critical dismissal re-check.
- `ReviewProcess::SinglePass(SinglePassReview)` accepts only `SinglePassPhase::{Acceptance, Review}`. Its constructor has no declaration or risk-class parameter, and its state has no constructible risk class, round/cap counter, streak, foreclosure, or convergence state. It still has an explicit `AwaitingRecheck` state: a high/critical dismissal blocks completion until an upheld result, while an overturned result routes the finding/shortfall as valid.

The top-level exhaustive dispatcher and each variant's transition are total for their own typed inputs. A convergence input cannot be applied to a single pass by fabricating null/zero fields, and a single-pass result cannot enter streak arithmetic. Metrics records may retain descriptive acceptance/review snapshot fields for append-only compatibility, but reconstruction does not transfer them into `SinglePassReview`.

THE BOUNDARY. No ready-frontier scheduling, parallel fan-out, record/write commands, persistent FSM store, or workflow DSL. `workflow-ready-frontier-scheduler` owns the first item; the others retain the YAGNI boundary in `workflow-driver`.

### Increment 1, `workflow-driver-typed-fleet-inc1`

RISK CLASS `risky` (two consecutive clean review rounds). The typed engine becomes the common source for checker and driver state and controls the instruction an agent receives. A divergence can either waive required review, fabricate convergence semantics for a single pass, or deadlock legal work.

ACCEPTANCE.

1. W3, Stage-1 `next`, and the fleet call the reviewed reconstruction delivered by `review-loop-foreclosure-enforcement` for ordered records, exact phase/escalation/adoption segments, authoritative Roadmap-increment/task-plan declarations, both historical classifications, active selection, snapshot agreement, streak, cap, foreclosure, and process kind. No second formula, declaration lookup, open-identity selector, phase join, or single-pass classifier exists under `src/driver/`. A differential suite replays logs and compares the fleet's enriched forward state with the earlier reconstruction and backward validator verdict.
2. `ReviewProcess::{Convergence, SinglePass}` is exhaustive and disjoint. `Convergence` alone can construct class/streak/foreclosure/cap fields; `SinglePass` has no such fields, constructor parameters, defaults, or accessors. Compile-fail/type-level red controls reject adding `Acceptance|Review` to `ConvergencePhase`, passing risk class into `SinglePassReview::open`, or reading a streak/cap from a single pass.
3. Both transition tables are total at compile time: every legal `(state, typed input)` pair has a defined result or typed rejection, and adding a process/state/input variant makes the exhaustive suite fail to compile until handled. Cross-variant inputs cannot be represented as a legal call.
4. High/critical dismissal re-check works in both variants without sharing convergence arithmetic. A single pass with such a dismissal enters `AwaitingRecheck`; upheld settles that pass, overturned yields a valid finding/shortfall, and neither route constructs/increments a streak or tests a cap. Lower dismissals and zero-findings passes settle directly.
5. Every output requiring judgment is reachable only from an input carrying that judgment. Red mutations that synthesize a clean verdict, risk class, re-check outcome, or human choice fail.
6. Reconstruction returns every task-, question-, and step-scoped explicit active unit and preserves each phase/review identity established by `workflow-loop-visibility`. Legal active plan-review fixtures include task and exact Roadmap-increment scopes; exact Roadmap-step history remains a non-active diagnostic and never becomes work review or an orphan. Acceptance/review stay task-scoped `SinglePassReview`; Q-68/Q-69-shaped exploration stays question-scoped with no Roadmap owner; delivery-step units retain exact increments; pending `ready-to-plan`/`blocked` remains a typed fallback until Stage 3. No lowest-order active-work filter sits below the fleet; singular selected action derives from explicit collection first and fallback only when no explicit unit is actionable.
7. The selected Q-58 carrier supplies non-derivable `awaiting`, artifact/diff, and currency data through a typed boundary. Missing, stale, and dirty states are explicit; heuristic prose parsing is forbidden unless the Q-58 decision explicitly selected and bounded a parser, in which case that exact parser is the typed boundary.
8. Golden human and JSON fixtures cover: pre-round risky task and increment plan review; digest-pinned non-active exact-step history; a mismatched low-risk snapshot; task-scoped acceptance and standalone review with high/critical re-check but no convergence fields; question exploration without Roadmap step; step exploration, implementation, and work review; a converged low-risk increment beside explicitly selected zero-round risky sibling; Q-81 plan-review segmentation; pending ready/blocked fallback; first review, fixes, reviewer continuation, convergence, foreclosure/cap escalation, missing/mismatched declarations, future exact-step-plan-review and unowned-work-review rejection, both bounded historical reconstructions, terminal states, and multiple simultaneous units. No fixture obtains a task phase through implementation status, changes recorded phase, borrows an unrelated step, or guesses identity from declaration order/latest round.
9. Existing W3/W4/W5 tests and Stage-1 output fixtures remain green or receive reviewed, documented contract migrations. No scheduler or write path appears in the diff.
10. README documents typed process/state reconstruction, phase-bearing identities, the absence of single-pass convergence fields, and unchanged advisory/read-only authority; `CHANGELOG.md` records the Stage-2 engine.
