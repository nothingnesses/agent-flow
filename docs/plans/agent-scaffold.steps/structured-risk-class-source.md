### `structured-risk-class-source`: make plan declarations the authoritative review-loop risk-class source

Decision `Q-78-risk-class-source` retired the ledger prose duplicate in favour of structured data. Q-83 settled the Roadmap increment arm: the human chose `Make the plan increment authoritative`, over making the first round authoritative or adding an authoritative loop-open event. This repair preserves that decision. It makes phase part of every live loop identity, supplies the corresponding task-plan declaration, and gives exact step-slug plan-review and non-Roadmap work-review history closed typed dispositions; none of those additions asks the human to choose authority again.

THE REPRODUCED DEFECTS. `[[step.increment]].risk_class` is already required and documented as setting an increment's convergence streak, but `PlanToml::step_views` drops increments, W3 reads the first round's class, and `next` reports no class before round one. A scratch TOML declaring `dangerous-inc1` as `risky` plus one clean round that snapshots `low_risk` currently exits zero from `validate --workflow`; the cheaper one-round bar wins over the declared two-round bar. Before the first round, the same declared increment reaches `next` with `risk_class` and `required_streak` null. The retained fieldless histories `round-log-core-incA` and `round-log-core-incB` cannot pass the current lowercase-only increment-id parser, task-level `plan_review` loops such as `q78-design-pass` have no increment in which to declare a class, and a multi-increment step gives `next` no durable fact identifying which declared sibling is open before that sibling has a round.

The committed log adds two phase/owner shapes that the earlier two-arm repair omitted. Sixty-three `plan_review` rounds resolve to eighteen Roadmap identities: exact step-slug identities for `structured-skeleton` and `checks-runner-worktree-name-collision`, three fieldless-increment identities (`decision-folder-currency-fold`, `workflow-enforcement-tier-fold`, and `workflow-enforcement-tier-endproperty-fold`), and the thirteen structured Q-81 increment identities. Eight `work_review` rounds resolve to no Roadmap step at all, at records 1, 47, 48, 164, 173, 177, 178, and 379. The append-only log is not rewritten, a Roadmap slug is not made an orphan task, and a recorded plan review is not relabelled as work review.

THE AUTHORITATIVE DECLARATION PROJECTION. Parse all live convergence declarations into one phase-bearing `LoopDeclaration` enum:

- `RoadmapIncrement { step, increment, phase, risk_class }` uses exact owned `[[step.increment]]` fields. Q-83's `risk_class` remains authoritative, and a new required `phase = "plan_review" | "work_review"` makes the declaration itself phase-bearing before round one. One increment id declares exactly one phase; changing phase requires a new increment identity rather than reinterpreting history. A round or active selection with the other phase is a mismatch, never a second loop silently sharing the class.
- `TaskPlanReview { task, phase = plan_review, risk_class }` uses one top-level `[[task_loop]]` row carrying exactly `task`, `phase`, and `risk_class`. Its key is the exact `(task, phase)` pair, duplicate keys are rejected, and `task` must equal `[meta].title` or resolve through `[meta].orphan_tasks`. `acceptance`, standalone `review`, and `work_review` are rejected in this table.

The declaration exists and is selected before the first reviewer is spawned. Every round joined to a live convergence loop must resolve to exactly one phase-bearing declaration and match its class; a missing declaration, unresolved or ambiguous join, or mismatch fails closed before convergence, foreclosure, or cap arithmetic. `round.risk_class` stays required as an auditable event snapshot, but it never chooses or lowers the threshold. Acceptance and standalone review snapshots never become a convergence declaration. No reader recovers a missing class from the ledger or the first round.

THE PHASE-PRESERVING ROUND MAPPING. Resolution parses phase first, then preserves the event's exact structured identity. A `plan_review` round carrying `step` plus `increment` resolves only to that owned Roadmap increment and keeps `plan_review`. A pre-migration round carrying `step` but no `increment` keeps `task` byte-for-byte as its review identity: if `task` equals an increment owned by that step, it resolves to the Roadmap-increment declaration; if `task` equals the step slug, it must match that step's exact migration-only history row; any other value fails. A fully fieldless pre-migration round may use the existing leading-slug shim only to find a candidate Roadmap owner, after which the same exact-match rules apply. A non-Roadmap `plan_review` resolves only through its exact task declaration. Structured ids win over the shim, no normalization or case-folding occurs, and an identity that could resolve more than one way is an error rather than a precedence guess.

A `work_review` round resolves to a live convergence loop only through an exact owned Roadmap increment declared with `phase = "work_review"`; it cannot enter `[[task_loop]]` or an exact-step history row. A phase-blind grouping by `(step, increment)` is forbidden: phase remains in the declaration/key through reconstruction, escalation segmentation, W3, `next`, and the later driver, and a record with the other phase fails rather than being counted.

ESCALATION PHASE. New convergence-loop escalation records carry `phase = "plan_review" | "work_review"` in addition to their exact task/step/increment identity. The current phase-less escalation records remain append-only: the migration shim may attach one only when its exact identity has exactly one candidate convergence phase, as the Q-81 escalations at records 421, 422, and 429 do; zero or multiple candidate phases fail. A phase-blind or ambiguous escalation never resets both loops. The metrics schema remains hand-writable and strict about new guidance; no event is fabricated or rewritten.

THE NARROW INCREMENT-ID COMPATIBILITY CONTRACT. Parse a declared increment id into an `IncrementId` type with exactly two accepted forms. `Canonical` is today's lowercase `is_kebab_case_token`. `LegacyFieldlessSuffix` is accepted only when the owning step slug is itself a canonical lowercase slug and the id is the exact byte string `<owner-step-slug>-inc<X>`, where `<X>` is one ASCII uppercase letter. The legacy arm is stored and compared without case-folding or normalization, is globally unique in the same exact-string namespace as canonical ids, and exists solely so fieldless append-only identities such as `round-log-core-incA` and `round-log-core-incB` can be declared and retain their existing joins. It does not widen step slugs, `blocked_by`, `folds`, question ids, task ids, or any other token class. It also does not admit uppercase in the owner/body, a multi-character suffix, a suffix followed by another component, underscores, non-ASCII, or an id whose owner prefix is a different step.

THE OPEN-LOOP OPERATION. A declaration is not an open-loop signal: this plan legitimately predeclares sibling increments and retained declarations. This increment therefore introduces the live convergence-bearing variants of the ordered `[[active_work]]` union. Task plan review carries its exact `task`. Roadmap increment review carries exact `step`, `increment`, and declared `phase`. Exact step-slug plan-review compatibility rows are retired history and cannot be selected. The orchestrator opens a live loop by authoring or selecting the declaration and committing the matching active-work row before round one. A declaration may have been prepared earlier, but selection freezes the exact phase/identity/class for the opened loop; predeclared siblings and the other phase remain closed. `next` and the shared active-loop reconstruction consume that selection and never guess from declaration order, lexical order, latest round, or first unconverged group. Historical retired loops need no fabricated active row. `workflow-loop-visibility` extends this same union with the non-convergence task/question/step phases and plural projection; it does not create a second selector.

THE CLOSED EXACT-STEP PLAN-REVIEW HISTORY. The seven retained rounds whose task is exactly a Roadmap step slug join one typed migration-only `[[step.historical_plan_review]]` declaration under each owning step. The declaration's identity and phase are fixed to that step slug plus `plan_review`; it carries `risk_class`, closed reason `predates-phase-bearing-loop-identity`, and non-empty `{ ordinal, sha256 }` selectors. It projects as `HistoricalReviewDeclaration::RoadmapStepPlanReview` and reconstructs `HistoricalRoadmapStepPlanReview`: exact outcomes/class remain available to legacy W3's peak-convergence check, but it cannot be active, reopened, inherited, waived, or used for foreclosure/cap because retained `structured-skeleton` has a later record after its first clean completion without a typed reopen boundary. A fresh step-slug plan-review round is invalid; future Roadmap plan review uses an exact increment declaration or task identity.

The current rows pin records 98, 99, and 115 for `structured-skeleton` (`b52c45969a73b4d14b0395a34ffb76ea3b28fa4ef88df7a2abbeb367111169d8`, `4cca71b0c8d4b9770d703f705ef5c4ee295b293e81527e14e0d17917af93c14b`, and `6eedf83fd23df3fb91d494bfa640edcd9d75ea9cee4da0be8e280ba4ed7f4a9f`) and records 217, 219, 220, and 221 for `checks-runner-worktree-name-collision` (`f08287fd68f433a7bd8861ecd75429c82e3ff26cf13759ece5d14f8ca3dd617e`, `e651aa5848a03fc19fe133f79a01597f302aad48d1d980ca1a0d6dd8c79d9ef6`, `b3b3e7921e895b582c056bf956dd8e8ded2d9213cf96bf887e9d067ea5fb08ec`, and `59c6da3ed7c1ed17a05b2cf6bf5742fec59ec0ad099581dc439b5c7b2b0de25b`). THE CLOSED NON-ROADMAP WORK-REVIEW HISTORY. Future `work_review` is legal only with an exact Roadmap increment declaration and selection. The eight retained non-Roadmap records instead join one typed migration-only `[[meta.historical_work_review]]` row per exact task. Each row has fixed `phase = "work_review"`, `risk_class`, closed reason `predates-roadmap-loop-identity`, and a non-empty list of `{ ordinal, sha256 }` selectors over raw JSON bytes without the line ending. It parses as `HistoricalUnownedWorkReview`, not `LoopDeclaration`: it cannot be active, cannot satisfy W3, and has no streak, foreclosure, cap, waiver, or task-loop authority. Every selected record must be a matching non-Roadmap round with the declared class; selectors, tasks, and ordinals are unique; every unowned historical work-review round must be selected exactly once. An unselected future record fails workflow validation rather than remaining accepted and unjoined.

The current plan receives exactly these historical rows and no broader cutoff: record 1 `workflow-hardening` (`95a14c80769d7607b026b9d92468996e8fa4e7856c8d92dd7797183594db1740`); records 47 and 48 `metrics-fields` (`608a5c72e49d6cdd7feec375547c7215901e9560317f6d7c8ae9955e608195a4` and `4cbcdbad96d39cc4934b8983cc24c767b320d6f565b8a986d1734e827b95ef63`); record 164 `q59-backlog-fold` (`1f35896ce152b381f036c916ede4a74ed8b6e2d37bca20e07abee98caa365316`); record 173 `decision-fold-q60-q62` (`9ad4639bcfd95f0c68d3628949833481cc7f46156c4613a48de5e8d5ba86beda`); record 177 `q64-capture` (`ae2389aaef1bb5f15028d91f56dd7fc38c79818e316be247da58d6071041d423`); record 178 `q65-capture` (`048889a8af0aa8414086836f705706a259ae3b5ad7f9f6c9256305f6c39a058c`); and record 379 `ship-v0-0-4` (`9f390aa306869809b9519fe0c85375a6214a01514aba55d5825357a061393c08`). The plan-source projection, workflow check, and forward driver use the one declaration/selection view. `src/plan/source.rs` retains each increment's typed exact id, owner, and class; parses `[[step.historical_plan_review]]`, `[[task_loop]]`, both typed historical categories, and the live convergence-bearing active-work variants; and rejects illegal cross-products. `src/metrics.rs` retains round phase and accepts the structured escalation phase. `src/workflow.rs` uses the phase-bearing view for W3 and shared reconstruction. `src/next.rs` uses the selected loop for pre-first-round output and rejects conflicting snapshots rather than selecting either value. Existing Roadmap histories migrate without changing events: the two exact step-slug histories gain digest-pinned `[[step.historical_plan_review]]` rows; all sixteen increment identities retain their existing `[[step.increment]]` class and gain the observed `plan_review` phase; every other declared increment gains its observed or intended work-review phase; task plan-review histories gain exact task declarations; and the eight unowned work-review records gain only their bounded historical rows. The append-only round log is not rewritten.

The ledger narrative keeps the review evidence a resuming human needs, but points at the plan declaration, selected phase-bearing active-work identity, and joined round records rather than restating class or required count. Narrative round/streak history remains until the separately open structured-ledger design settles its own scope; this step removes only the prose duplicate and settles the relationship among declarations, active selection, event snapshots, and retained history.
