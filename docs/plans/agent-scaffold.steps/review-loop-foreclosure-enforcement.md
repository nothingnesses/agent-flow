### `review-loop-foreclosure-enforcement`: report and enforce review-loop foreclosure and the total-round cap

Decision `Q-78-foreclosure-enforcement` chose `Report and enforce foreclosure plus the round cap`, over report-only and keeping both advisory. The choice already exists in the event log; this step gives it focused Roadmap ownership after the structured class/selection, TOML-waiver guidance, and rebuild-boundary rules land.

THE PROBLEM. `WorkflowSpec` carries `round_cap`, and `next` reports escalation only after a loop reaches it. `validate --workflow` does not enforce the cap. More sharply, a convergence loop can become unable to reach its required consecutive-clean streak before the cap: with rounds used `r`, current clean streak `c`, cap `k`, and required streak `q`, foreclosure holds when `k - r < q - c`. Running another review in that state spends a round without any possible converging outcome.

THE SHARED RECONSTRUCTION. Extract one ordered, typed review-process reconstruction used by `validate --workflow` and `next`. Round and escalation records retain their one-based JSONL record ordinal. The top-level result is a disjoint enum, not one struct with optional arithmetic:

- `ReviewProcessHistory::Convergence(LoopWindow)` is the only variant with an authoritative `LoopDeclaration`, `risk_class`, required streak, current streak, round count, escalation segments, convergence, foreclosure, and cap. Its identity is phase-bearing. `RoadmapIncrement(step, increment, declared phase)` and `TaskPlanReview(task, plan_review)` are distinct exact keys; an event with the other phase mismatches rather than sharing a window.
- `ReviewProcessHistory::SinglePass(SinglePassHistory)` covers `acceptance` and standalone `review`. It preserves exact task/artifact and re-check state, but its type has no risk class, required/current streak, round count for cap purposes, foreclosure, cap segment, or convergence declaration. The required high/critical dismissal re-check remains part of this variant: an applicable dismissal leaves it awaiting re-check until upheld or overturned.
- `ReviewProcessHistory::HistoricalRoadmapIncrementPlanReview`, `HistoricalRoadmapStepPlanReview`, and `HistoricalUnownedWorkReview` are bounded migration types supplied by `structured-risk-class-source`. The first preserves Q-81's 41 digest-pinned exact-increment plan-review rounds and three digest-pinned escalation boundaries while leaving the same ids' live declarations to work review; the second preserves only the seven digest-pinned exact-step plan-review records and their legacy peak-convergence result; the third joins only the eight digest-pinned non-Roadmap work-review records. None is active or enters live loop-window arithmetic.

A scoped escalation is a segment boundary only for one exact `Convergence` key: later rounds for that exact phase-bearing unit start a new counter window, while prior rounds remain history. New escalation records carry phase. A retained phase-less escalation for a live loop maps only when its task/step/increment identity has exactly one candidate convergence phase; otherwise it is an ambiguity error, never a reset of all same-string loops. Q-81's phase-less escalations at records 421, 422, and 429 instead match only their exact digest-pinned `HistoricalRoadmapIncrementPlanReview` selectors, segment only those closed `plan_review` histories, and cannot reset the live `work_review` declarations that share the increment tokens.

Every live convergence identity reads the authoritative class from the declaration substrate delivered by `structured-risk-class-source`: a Roadmap increment loop resolves its owned `[[step.increment]]`, and a task plan review resolves its unique `[[task_loop]]`. The reconstruction requires every snapshot to match, then computes rounds used, current clean streak, required streak, cap, and foreclosure from that declaration, the ordered events, and `WorkflowSpec`. A recorded window with a missing declaration, phase ambiguity, or mismatch, and an asserted active window with a missing/unresolved selection, is checked before any convergence arithmetic; convergence is then checked before foreclosure and cap, preserving the existing convergence-first precedence. Retired historical convergence windows need no active-work row. No single-pass or historical variant can call foreclosure/cap computations; only the exact-step historical variant exposes its separately bounded legacy peak result to W3.

ENFORCEMENT BOUNDARY. `next` reports `escalate` as soon as the selected active enforced convergence window is foreclosed or reaches the cap without convergence, with a reason that distinguishes the two. `validate --workflow` rejects a foreclosed active window with no following scoped escalation, a cap-reached unconverged window with no following scoped escalation, and any enforced segment containing more rounds than the cap. A joined escalation closes that violation and resets the next segment. Historical rounds before a real escalation remain history and never count against its following segment. Acceptance/review complete their one reviewers-then-triager pass, subject to the high/critical dismissal re-check, and never enter this enforcement arithmetic.
