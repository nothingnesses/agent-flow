## 1. Why this is a separate pass

`Q-78`'s brief directed four subjects as one schema, and `Q-78.md` section 3 gave a mechanical reason for the grouping: typed umbrella membership forces `render` to emit the member headings from data, so the sidecar stops owning the heading, so item 4 decides duty (g) as a consequence.

ROUND 2 MEASURED THAT REASON FALSE THREE WAYS, so umbrella membership carried no stated reason to sit in that pass.

- The recommended design declines to generate member headings. Each member heading stays in the member's own file, promoted in place.
- The member headings never sat in the first member's sidecar. Each sits in its own file. Reproduce with `head -1 docs/plans/agent-scaffold.steps/available-filter.md`.
- The group heading that this design DOES generate restates no `[[step]]` field, because an umbrella heading names no slug. So typed membership removes no restatement of a step field, and duty (g) asks exactly whether a sidecar may restate a `[[step]]` field.

The human decided on 2026-08-21 to split the subject into its own pass, over keeping it in `Q-78`. The decision receipt is `type:"decision"` `q_id:"Q-78-umbrellascope"` in `docs/metrics/workflow.jsonl`.

THE WORK IS A REPAIR AND NOT A PRECAUTION, which is why the split defers it rather than drops it. Three positional claims across two umbrella sidecars are false on the tree today, with no reorder involved. Section 2 gives them.

## 2. The evidence

### Adjacency is already false, not merely fragile

Four of the five umbrella sidecars assert adjacency in the words "the N steps below". Two of those four assertions are false today.

- `convergence-accounting` claims eight steps below. Its members render at 13, 14, 15, 16, 20, 23, 24 and 27. Non-members sit inside that span at 17 (`file-safety-rules`), 18 (`agent-isolation`), 19 (`user-prompts-dir`), 21 (`gate-prompt-clarity`), 22 (`compaction-prep`), 25 (`no-wrap-convention`) and 26 (`findings-files`).
- `file-safety-rules` claims four steps below. Its members render at 17, 18, 19 and 22, split by `gate-prompt-clarity` at 21. `file-safety-rules` and its members at 18, 19 and 22 all sit inside the `convergence-accounting` span, so two umbrellas overlap.
- `file-safety-rules` also states that `findings-files` is "above". `findings-files` renders at 26 and `file-safety-rules` renders at 17, so `findings-files` sits nine rows below.

Two assertions hold: `mode-enum` at 5, 6, 7 and 8, and `pack-manifest` at 9, 10 and 11.

One positional claim in the same set is TRUE and is stated so the sweep is not read as uniform: `convergence-accounting` says `state-schema` follows immediately after `ledger-template`, and `ledger-template` renders at 27 with `state-schema` at 28.

A SECOND DEFECT SITS IN THE SAME SENTENCE AND IS NOT POSITIONAL. `convergence-accounting.md:3` calls `state-schema` "a separate, deferred item", and `state-schema` is `complete`. Reproduce with:

```
awk '/^\[\[step\]\]/{s=""} /^slug = /{s=$3} /^status = /{print s, $3}' docs/plans/agent-scaffold.plan.toml | grep state-schema
```

The `Q-78` pass inspected this sentence, certified the positional half and did not report the status half. A later pass must decide whether the false adjective goes with the positional sweep or with `sidecar-status-opening-drift`, whose selector does not reach it (section 5, item 7).

### The fifth umbrella decides the design

`exploration-mode` asserts no adjacency. Its member list at `docs/plans/agent-scaffold.steps/exploration-mode.md:3` names `exploration-mode`, `ledger-parse`, `workflow-invariants` and `state-queries`. They render at 37, 40, 41 and 46. Six non-members sit inside that span: `session-preflight` 38, `round-log-core` 39, `escalation-exempt` 42, `decision-receipt` 43, `waiver-model` 44 and `structured-skeleton` 45.

So the plan carries a `complete`, genuine and useful umbrella whose member set is NOT contiguous and which asserts no positional claim. That refutes, from the project's own corpus, the premise a contiguity rule rests on. A non-contiguous member set is not an illegal state in this plan. It is what a shared-context grouping looks like once other work lands between its members. An umbrella here means SHARED CONTEXT and not a positional claim, so the illegal state to remove is "prose that asserts a position", and typed membership plus a generated heading removes it whether or not the members sit together.

THE NARROW CLAIM IS THE LOAD-CARRYING ONE, and a wider claim in the `Q-78` draft was false. That draft said `exploration-mode`'s "prose asserts nothing false". Its prose asserts two false STATUS claims: `:3` says "`workflow-invariants` (next)" on a step whose declared `status` is `complete`, and `:7` opens "Next." on a `complete` step. A stale status token bears on nothing about contiguity, because it would be equally stale if the four members sat at 37, 38, 39 and 40. What the argument needs is that `exploration-mode` makes no false POSITIONAL claim, and that holds.

## 3. The form the `Q-78` pass recommended

- A `[[umbrella]]` array of tables, each with `slug` and `title`.
- An `umbrella` field on `[[step]]`, holding an umbrella slug. `validate` resolves the reference fail-closed, in the same manner as `blocked_by` and `folds`.
- NO CONTIGUITY RULE. `validate` says nothing about where the members sit.
- `render` emits one group per CONTIGUOUS RUN of the `umbrella` value. The umbrella's title and its shared context render on the FIRST run. A later run of the same umbrella renders the title with a generated continuation marker and no repeated context.
- The shared context lives at `docs/plans/agent-scaffold.umbrellas/<slug>.md` and is OPTIONAL. An absent file means no context paragraph and is not an error.

WHY THE `umbrella` FIELD STAYS, stated because a round 1 reviewer proposed deleting it as a second copy of a fact the array already carries. It is not a second copy. `mode-enum` occupies rendered 5 to 8 and `pack-manifest` occupies 9 to 11. Position alone cannot tell a reader where one run ends and the next begins, so two adjacent runs are indistinguishable from one longer run. The field carries the PARTITION, and position does not carry the partition at all. That is the difference from `order`, which WAS fully derivable from declaration position.

WHY NO CONTIGUITY RULE. Section 2 gives the corpus refutation. A contiguity rule also prices the exact operation `Q-78` item 3 exists to make cheap: cutting a member out of its run makes the plan invalid, and pasting any step into an umbrella's span makes the plan invalid for a reason unrelated to the step being moved. A step's legal positions would then depend on other steps' `umbrella` values, which is a non-local constraint over the whole array.

THE ENCODABLE ALTERNATIVE WAS PRICED AND REJECTED. `Q-78` section 6 rejects an explicit slug list on a stated rule: when an ENCODABLE alternative exists, prefer it to a CHECKABLE one. The encodable form of umbrella membership is nested groups, one array of groups each with its own nested step array, with a run of ungrouped steps as an untitled group. Under nested groups a non-contiguous member set is not writable at all. It is rejected because it does not remove the reorder, it makes the reorder MANDATORY, and because every consumer must flatten (`src/plan/source.rs`, `src/plan/render.rs`, `src/next.rs`, `src/workflow.rs`, `src/metrics.rs`), every fixture and every inline TOML literal in the test suite changes shape, the Markdown substrate in `src/plan.rs` owes a parity story it does not have, and every ungrouped step acquires membership of a synthetic untitled group. So the contiguity rule does not fall to `Q-78`'s own test. It falls to the corpus, because the state it forbids is a state the plan holds today and holds legitimately.

WHY ONE GROUP PER RUN RATHER THAN A BACK-REFERENCE. A variant that keeps one group and emits a pointer at each later member was rejected under Principle 2, Minimal by default: a pointer is render machinery added to describe a split. A group per run needs no pointer and no new schema, because the run boundary is already in the data.

WHY THE CONTEXT SIDECAR IS OPTIONAL. `render_plan` (`src/plan/render.rs:135`) collects every unreadable sidecar and returns an error, so it produces no output at all. Every question sidecar is 0 bytes and exists only to satisfy that check, which is a wart in the reader's contract. A required umbrella sidecar imports the same wart for a third file class, and a scaffolded project declaring an umbrella with no shared context must then create an empty file or get no render. One optional clause avoids that, and it does not require fixing the existence contract for the other two classes, which stays a separate step.

## 4. What the recommended form costs and buys

THE COST, CORRECTED. The `Q-78` draft said that `convergence-accounting`, `file-safety-rules` and `exploration-mode` "each render their title more than once today". That is false in the present tense. Each of the five umbrella titles renders EXACTLY ONCE today:

```
grep -n '^### ' docs/plans/agent-scaffold.md | grep -iE 'workflow hardening|durability, recovery|TUI polish|bring-your-own|workflow self-improvement'
```

A repeated title is a property the recommended design INTRODUCES. The measured cost is that the document goes from 5 group headings to 11, of which 6 are continuations. Take each umbrella's member list from its owner's sidecar and resolve each slug with the position command above:

- `convergence-accounting`: 13,14,15,16 | 20 | 23,24 | 27. Four runs.
- `file-safety-rules`: 17,18,19 | 22. Two runs.
- `exploration-mode`: 37 | 40,41 | 46. Three runs.
- `mode-enum`: 5,6,7,8. One run.
- `pack-manifest`: 9,10,11. One run.

These five figures do not expire as the plan grows, because all five clusters are `complete` or `skipped` or `deferred` work in the middle of the plan and no append moves their run structure. A later insert inside one of the spans does move them, and the command above is what re-derives them.

THE COST IS JUDGED A FEATURE. A contiguity rule hides the same drift behind a validator error, and the cheapest way for an author to clear that error is to delete the `umbrella` field from the step being moved, which silently destroys the membership fact. A visible continuation marker states the truth. A validator error trains the author to erase it.

THE BUY.

- Array-position authority stays intact, so the umbrella and the plan order stop contradicting each other.
- Reprioritisation stays one cut and paste for every step in the plan, which is the human's stated requirement.
- No illegal state is invented, so Principle 5, Make illegal states unrepresentable, is not misapplied in either direction.
- The three false positional claims are deleted rather than corrected, because the position becomes structural (Principle 8, Structured data first, project for humans).

## 5. What the specification still owes

Round 2 read the `Q-78` acceptance criteria for the first time and found seven defects in the umbrella increment. Each is recorded with its measurement, so a later pass repairs the specification rather than rediscovers the defect. None of the seven touches the design in section 3.

1. NO CRITERION FORCED THE DATA TO EXIST. The eight criteria of the `Q-78` umbrella increment named neither `docs/plans/agent-scaffold.plan.toml`, nor `[[umbrella]]`, nor the `umbrella` field on a live step. The cheapest implementation that passed all eight built the schema and the render support, deleted the five umbrella heading blocks and the positional sentences, promoted the member headings, and left the live plan with zero umbrella data. One criterion required prose DELETED "that the `[[umbrella]]` data now carries", so with no umbrella data no sentence qualified and that criterion went vacuous. The five shared-context paragraphs are not recoverable from the projection, because `render --check --strict` passes against a projection regenerated from the diminished source. THE SPECIFICATION OWES a criterion naming the five umbrella slugs and asserting mechanically that the set of `[[umbrella]]` slugs equals `{convergence-accounting, file-safety-rules, mode-enum, pack-manifest, exploration-mode}`, that for each the set of steps carrying that `umbrella` value equals the member list in section 2, and that `docs/plans/agent-scaffold.umbrellas/<slug>.md` exists and is non-empty for each of the five with the moved prose.
2. THE CONTINUATION MARKER IS UNSPECIFIED, AND IT IS THE VISIBLE PRODUCT OF THE DESIGN. Five sites in the `Q-78` artefact carried the bare phrase "a generated continuation marker" and none said what it is. Unspecified: its text, whether it names the umbrella or the run index, whether it points back at the first run, whether it is part of the heading string or a separate line, and its heading LEVEL. No test can be written from that. THE SPECIFICATION OWES the exact rendered form of a first run and of a continuation run, as literal text, pinned in the golden fixture.
3. THE GROUP HEADING'S LEVEL IS UNDECIDED AND THE REPOSITORY HOLDS TWO CONTRADICTORY PRECEDENTS. `mode-enum.md` and `pack-manifest.md` carry the umbrella at `###` and their own step heading at `####`, so their members nest. `convergence-accounting.md`, `file-safety-rules.md` and `exploration-mode.md` carry both at `###`, so their members sit at the same level. A criterion that forces every STEP heading to one level resolves neither precedent, and the increment adds the group heading as a new class into the same crowded level. Reproduce the two precedents with `grep -n '^#' docs/plans/agent-scaffold.steps/mode-enum.md | head -2` and the same for `convergence-accounting.md`. THE SPECIFICATION OWES the level, stated.
4. THE ROADMAP TABLE IS UNADDRESSED. `roadmap_section` (`src/plan/render.rs:465`) emits one row per step with no grouping concept. Nothing states whether it gains one. THE SPECIFICATION OWES a ruling.
5. `[[umbrella]]` MINTS A NEW ID CLASS AND INHERITS NONE OF THE RULES THE OTHER ID CLASSES CARRY. Every id class in `src/plan/source.rs` carries a duplicate rule and two carry a well-formedness rule: step slug (`:529`, `:532`), increment id (`:542`, `:547`), question id (`:559`), waiver id (`:572`), principle (`:581`) and meta orphan task (`:764`). A duplicate umbrella slug makes the step reference ambiguous and `render`'s title lookup non-deterministic, and nothing rules on whether an unreferenced umbrella is an error. Principle 5 is the principle the increment cited most and it was not applied to the id the increment mints. THE SPECIFICATION OWES a duplicate rule, a kebab-case rule, each with a red-then-green test, and a ruling on the unreferenced umbrella.
6. THE OPTIONAL-SIDECAR CRITERION IS WEAK AT ITS FIRST CLAUSE. "`render` produces the same output for an umbrella with a context sidecar and for one with no sidecar at all, apart from the context paragraph" CAN fail, so it is not vacuous, but it carries no command and no golden pin, and "apart from the context paragraph" lets an implementer attribute a stray blank line to the missing paragraph. THE SPECIFICATION OWES a golden pin: an umbrella with no context file renders its group heading and its members and emits no empty paragraph.
7. THE PROSE WORKLIST IS SHORT BY ONE FILE AND THE HEADING WORKLIST BY TWO. `docs/plans/agent-scaffold.documentation-protocol.md:7` states the umbrella convention in prose: "some detail blocks are grouped under an umbrella heading that carries context shared by several steps, and the umbrella itself is not a Roadmap entry". After the increment the umbrella heading comes from `[[umbrella]]` data and stops being a hand-authored convention, so that clause goes stale. It sat on no worklist, and both `Q-78` worklists were declared closed, so nothing would have brought it forward. The heading worklist's two missing files are `mode-enum.md` and `pack-manifest.md` (section 2). THE SPECIFICATION OWES both additions. Note that the `documentation-protocol.md:7` REPRIORITISATION clause is a different clause in the same sentence and belongs to `plan-order-array-position` increment 2, which carries it.

TWO FURTHER ITEMS THIS PASS INHERITS, neither a criteria defect.

- DUTY (g)'S HEADING HALF. `Q-78` duty (g) rules that a sidecar must not restate a `[[step]]` field, and excludes the heading, naming the heading as the thing `render` takes over later. The generated step heading is the end state and no pass schedules it. A typed umbrella must ship before it, to show whether the grouping is enough on its own.
- THE SELECTOR GAP AT `exploration-mode`. `sidecar-status-opening-drift`'s selector reads the first non-blank non-heading line of the FILE. In the five umbrella-owner files that line is the umbrella paragraph, not the step's own opening, so a status token in a step opening BELOW an umbrella paragraph is outside that step's scope. `exploration-mode.md:7` is one such token. Once the umbrella prose moves to `docs/plans/agent-scaffold.umbrellas/<slug>.md`, the five owner files start at their own step opening and the selector reaches them. So this increment makes the drift step's scope complete as a side effect, and that is worth a criterion.

## 6. The optional tidy-up, priced and not required

Under the recommended form nothing has to move. If the human wants the clusters to read contiguously, the change reaches TWO disjoint regions of the published document:

- The span from `convergence-accounting` to `ledger-template`, which holds the interleaved `convergence-accounting` and `file-safety-rules` clusters. Every step in that span is `complete`.
- The span from `exploration-mode` to `state-queries`, which holds the `exploration-mode` cluster. `ledger-parse` and `state-queries` are `skipped` and `escalation-exempt` is `deferred`, so "all complete" is true of the first span only.

Both spans are named by their endpoint slugs rather than by a row count, because a row count expires as soon as the plan grows and a slug does not. Measure the current size of either span with the position command above. The cost of DECLINING the tidy-up is the six continuation headings section 4 measures.

The tidy-up stays inert for `next` either way. `select_active_loop` (`src/next.rs:709`) takes an `InProgress` step first and then a pending one, and `is_pending` (`src/next.rs:499`) is `NotStarted | Next`. No step in either span sits in any of those phases, so no reorder of either span changes what `next` selects.

The tidy-up falsifies no chronology, and the reason is measured rather than assumed: plan order is already not build order. `validation-constraints` is `not-started` and renders AHEAD of `ship-v0-0-2`, which is `complete`. Check the pair with the position command above, and note that no row number is given for it deliberately, because neither number carries any part of the argument and both move as soon as a step is inserted earlier.
